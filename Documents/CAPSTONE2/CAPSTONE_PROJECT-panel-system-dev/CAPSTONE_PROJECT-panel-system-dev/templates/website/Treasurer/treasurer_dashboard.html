Help & Guide

Step-by-step workflows for your role — follow the numbered steps in order.

Add Members

1. Go to Members in the sidebar
2. Click "Add Member" and fill in their details
3. A Membership Fee record is auto-created
4. Collect the fee → goes to Auditor → President for approval

Monthly Dues

1. Go to Monthly Dues → By Salary Deduction
2. Select month and upload the accounting sheet
3. Submit batch → Auditor verifies → President approves
4. Fund inflow recorded automatically upon approval

Aids — Prerequisite

1 File aid at Aid Requests → Medical Aid or Death Aid Claim
↓
2 AUDITOR verifies → PRESIDENT approves → AidTrackingPost created
↓
3 Go to Record Contribution tab and choose a workflow below

Normal Pay — Flow

TREASURER
1 Click Pay per member or use checkbox → Pay Selected → sets to RECORDED
↓
2 Use Skip for members who won't pay | Notify to send reminders
↓
3 Upload Deduction Sheet (file + batch reference + payroll period) at bottom panel
↓
4 Record Remittance (salary deduction posts only) — log when deducted amounts are deposited to CAUFA's account
↓
5 Click Mark as Finished (requires ≥70% RECORDED/PAID + deduction sheet uploaded)
↓
AUDITOR → PRESIDENT
6 Auditor batch-verifies → all RECORDED become PAID → fund inflow recorded → President approves
↓
TREASURER
7 Click Release → post closed → Auditor & President get notified with Acknowledge button

Paid with Funds — Flow

TREASURER
1 Click Paid with Funds → request sent to Auditor
↓
AUDITOR → PRESIDENT
2 Auditor verifies → President approves → post enters pending_release
↓
TREASURER
3 Click Release → OUTFLOW recorded (fund disburses) → post enters repayment phase
↓
TREASURER
4 Members pay individually via Pay buttons (sets RECORDED) to replenish the fund
↓
5 Click Close Repayment → remaining unpaid auto-skipped → sent to Auditor
↓
AUDITOR → PRESIDENT
6 Auditor batch-verifies → all RECORDED become PAID → INFLOW recorded → President approves → post closed
