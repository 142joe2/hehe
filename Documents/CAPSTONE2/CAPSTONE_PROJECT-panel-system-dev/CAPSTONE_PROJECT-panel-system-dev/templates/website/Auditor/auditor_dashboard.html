Help & Guide

Step-by-step workflows for your role — follow the numbered steps in order.

Payments Auditing

1. Go to Audit → Payments Auditing in the sidebar
2. Review Membership Fees & Monthly Dues submitted by Treasurer
3. Click Verify to approve or Return for revision
4. Once verified → sent to President for final approval

Aid Claims Auditing

1. Go to Audit → Aid Claims Auditing in the sidebar
2. Review Medical Aid / Death Aid requests and documents
3. Verify the request → sent to President for approval
4. President approves → AidTrackingPost created for member collection

Finish Verifications

1. Go to Verified Registry → Finish Verifications
2. Review Treasurer's close-out of contribution collection
3. Click Verify → all RECORDED become PAID → fund inflow recorded
4. President approves → post enters pending_release
5. Treasurer releases → you receive a notification with Acknowledge button to confirm
