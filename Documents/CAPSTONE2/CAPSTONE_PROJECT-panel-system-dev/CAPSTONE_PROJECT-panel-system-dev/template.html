About the Organization

The Isabela State University Cauayan Faculty Association (CAUFA) is the official faculty organization of ISU Cauayan, focused on representing members, strengthening collaboration, and supporting institutional goals.

Mission: To promote unity, professional growth, and meaningful faculty participation through responsible service and coordinated activities.

Vision: To be a trusted faculty organization that fosters excellence, integrity, and active engagement within the university community.
